About

The Avengers

The Avengers are a team of the world's most powerful superheroes, dedicated to protecting Earth from cosmic and terrestrial threats.

Founded by Nick Fury during a time of global crisis, the Avengers bring together exceptional individuals with extraordinary abilities. From genius billionaires to Norse gods, each member contributes unique strengths to the team. Together, they have faced threats that no single hero could withstand.

With headquarters at Avengers Compound, the team operates around the clock, ready to respond to any threat. Their bond transcends differences - united by a shared commitment to justice and the greater good.
